# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. This project exists to help people defend their own systems.

- Assume good faith; keep discussion technical and constructive.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination.
- Do not use this project, its issues, or its PRs to coordinate attacks on
  systems you do not own or are not authorized to test.

Report unacceptable behavior by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer
via the repository. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and
comments that violate this code.
